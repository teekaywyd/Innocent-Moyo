Who He Is

Educator, Leader & Mentor

Background

Mr Innocent Moyo began his educational journey at Hillside Teachers College, University of Zimbabwe, where he earned his Diploma in Education with a focus on Geography and Art & Design.

He later pursued his Bachelor of Education at UNISA, specialising in Geography & the Environment and English Home Language — completing it in 2020 while actively teaching.

His multilingual proficiency and deep subject knowledge have made him a respected figure across multiple institutions in both Zimbabwe and South Africa.

Skills & Expertise

Beyond the classroom, Mr Moyo is recognised for his leadership and strategic vision in educational management — a quality that led to his appointment as Principal at Crownfield Schools.

He also serves as a part-time tutor at Jacaranda International in Centurion, delivering the Cambridge International curriculum at both Ordinary and Advanced level.
